Data Management System

An 11-module system for tracking vaccine coverage and nutrition, designed to work for nurses in the field and managers in the office.

Illustration, not real data

Organisation
Rural Health Care Initiative (RHCI), Bo District
Role
System design and documentation
Status
Designed
Stack
PostgreSQL, Flask, progressive web app (PWA)
Users
Administrators and nurses, with role-based access

What it covers

The first scope, written in November 2024, was a malaria vaccine coverage and nutrition tracking system. It includes:

  • Patient management
  • Vaccine and nutrition tracking
  • Appointments and reminders
  • Missed appointment tracking
  • Dashboards, graphs and reports
  • Real-time notifications

How the design changed

The first plan used SQL Server, ASP.NET Core and React. In January 2025 the database moved to PostgreSQL, and the system settled into its current Flask and PWA design. A PWA suits low connectivity because it installs on a phone and keeps working with a weak signal.

Documentation

I produced planning documents across several versions, covering the modules, roles and data model, so a developer or team can build from them.
