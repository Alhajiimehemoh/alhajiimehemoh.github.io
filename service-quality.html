Data quality audits

Before numbers reach a report, the records behind them should be checked. I review your dataset for the errors that matter most and give each team clear, fair feedback.

What you get

  • A cleaned dataset and an issue log
  • Feedback workbooks per team or worker
  • A summary report for management
  • Checks you can repeat every month

Good for: Datasets you are about to report or rely on

Common questions

Will you change our data?

Only after an issue is confirmed, and every change is recorded in a cleaning log so it can be traced.

Can our team run the checks themselves later?

Yes. I can turn the checks into a monthly routine with a checklist and a log.

How it works

  1. Receive a safe export

    You send an export with identifying details removed or protected.

  2. Run the checks

    Completeness, duplicates, GPS, dose or visit sequences and name consistency.

  3. Confirm and clean

    Flags are confirmed before anything changes, and every correction is logged.

  4. Report back

    A cleaned dataset, feedback per team and a summary for management.
