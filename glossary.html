Glossary

Plain-language definitions of terms used in health data and M&E work.

CHW
Community Health Worker. A trained community member who delivers basic health services and refers people to facilities.
DHIS2
District Health Information Software 2. An open source platform many countries use to collect and analyse routine health data.
DHMT
District Health Management Team. The group that manages health services in a district.
EPI
Expanded Programme on Immunization. The programme that delivers routine childhood vaccines.
HMIS
Health Management Information System. The routine system for collecting health facility data.
KPI
Key Performance Indicator. A measure used to track progress toward a goal.
M&E
Monitoring and Evaluation. Tracking activities during a programme and judging its results afterwards.
MMS
Multiple Micronutrient Supplements, given to pregnant women to support health.
PHC
Primary Health Care. First-level facilities such as community health posts and centres.
Survey123
An ArcGIS tool for building forms that collect data on phones and tablets, including offline.
XLSForm
A standard for writing forms in a spreadsheet. Works with Survey123, KoboToolbox and ODK.
Data quality
How complete, timely, accurate and consistent data is. See the free checklist on the resources page.
