When QR codes help in registration and tracking systems

QR codes are useful for fast, accurate lookup, but only when the system behind them is designed properly.

21 October 2026 · Data collection · 1 min read

A QR code is a quick way to open a record without typing. It helps when a printed card, register or box needs to connect to digital data.

Good uses

  • Staff or community health worker cards that can be checked online
  • Stock items or boxes that need to be counted or issued
  • Attendance at training sessions or outreach events
  • Linking a paper form to the right digital record

What the code should contain

Keep the code to a short ID or web link. Do not put names, phone numbers or other personal details inside the code, because anyone with a phone can read it.

Plan for problems

  • Print codes large enough to scan on a basic phone
  • Protect printed cards from water and wear
  • Keep a way to look up the record by typing the ID
  • Decide who can see what once the code is scanned

A QR code is only the key. The reliable part is the database and the rules behind it.
