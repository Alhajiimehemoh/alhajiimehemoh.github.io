Data and M&E

Priced by scope. Send me your reporting need and I will reply with a plan and a quote.

  • Dashboards and reports

    Power BI and Excel dashboards that refresh from your survey exports, with views for field staff, managers and donors.

  • Survey and form design

    Survey123 XLSForms with lookups, skip logic, validation and QR autofill, tested before field use.

  • Data quality audits

    Checks for duplicates, GPS errors, impossible dose sequences, inconsistent village names and missing records, with a report per team or facility.

  • M&E reporting

    Monthly and quarterly programme reports, indicator tracking and presentations for management, boards and donors.

  • Data systems

    Database and web tool design for tracking patients, supplies, staff or credentials.

  • Training

    Hands-on training in Excel, data entry, data quality and basic dashboards for programme and facility staff.

How a project runs

  1. Assess

    We agree what you need and who will use it.

  2. Design

    I propose the form, system or setup and a fixed scope.

  3. Build and test

    I deliver it and test it with your real data or equipment.

  4. Hand over

    Documentation and training, so your team can keep it running.

Common questions

Where do you work?

Onsite in Bo and nearby areas, and remotely across Sierra Leone. Most data and dashboard work can be done remotely.

How much does a data project cost?

IT support packages are priced in Leones above. Data and M&E projects are priced by scope after a short conversation. Use the request form to describe what you need and I will reply with a plan and a quote.

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of what you report on, who reads the report, and a sample of your current forms or data exports. Remove names, phone numbers and other identifying details from any sample you send.

Which tools do you use?

Survey123 and XLSForm for data collection, Power BI and Excel for dashboards and reports, PostgreSQL and Flask for larger systems, and Google Apps Script for lightweight tools. I can also advise on choosing between KoboToolbox, CommCare, ODK and DHIS2.

How do you handle sensitive data?

Your data stays with you. I work from exports you control, and I never publish identifiable patient or staff information on this website.

Do you train our staff?

Yes. I run hands-on training in Excel, data entry, data quality checks and basic dashboards for programme and facility staff.

How quickly do you reply?

WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach me. Email suits longer documents and proposals.

Ready to start?

Describe the problem in a few lines. I will tell you what I would do and what it costs.
