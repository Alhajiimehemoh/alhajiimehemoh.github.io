Data and M&E

Priced by scope. Send me your reporting need and I will reply with a plan and a quote.

  • Dashboards and reports

    Power BI and Excel dashboards that refresh from your survey exports, with views for field staff, managers and donors.

  • Survey and form design

    Survey123 XLSForms with lookups, skip logic, validation and QR autofill, tested before field use.

  • Data quality audits

    Checks for duplicates, GPS errors, impossible dose sequences, inconsistent village names and missing records, with a report per team or facility.

  • M&E reporting

    Monthly and quarterly programme reports, indicator tracking and presentations for management, boards and donors.

  • Data systems

    Database and web tool design for tracking patients, supplies, staff or credentials.

  • Training

    Hands-on training in Excel, data entry, data quality and basic dashboards for programme and facility staff.

IT support

Onsite in Bo and nearby areas. Remote support across Sierra Leone.

  • Technical support and hardware

    Repairs, RAM and SSD upgrades, software installation, virus removal, printer and scanner setup.

  • Networking and internet

    LAN and Wi-Fi setup, routers, network design and cable management for offices, NGOs and clinics.

  • Web and digital

    WordPress sites, domains and hosting, maintenance, professional email and basic graphics.

  • Security and backups

    Backup and recovery, antivirus and firewall setup, user accounts, access control and system checks.

  • Office and NGO IT support

    Support contracts, staff training, ICT consultancy and inventory tools.
