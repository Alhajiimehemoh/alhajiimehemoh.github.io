Getting the most from them

  1. Start with one

    Pick the checklist that matches your next deadline and use it this month.

  2. Adapt the words

    Change labels and examples to your programme. Keep the structure.

  3. Remove personal data

    Never add patient names, phone numbers or ID numbers to shared files.

  4. Make it routine

    Same file, same day, every month. Consistency is what improves data.
