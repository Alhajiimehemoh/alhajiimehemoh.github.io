Survey123 forms and field tools

Forms and small tools that field and facility staff can use without training manuals, and that produce data a dashboard can trust.

Organisation
Rural Health Care Initiative (RHCI), Bo District
Role
Form design, build and testing
Status
Built
Tools
Survey123 (XLSForm), Excel, HTML
Audience
Field staff, facility staff, programme managers

Hospitalisation notification form

A Survey123 form for staff hospitalisation notices, with cascading lookups. Choosing an employee limits the dependent list to that employee, which prevents mismatched records.

Malaria campaign registration and field visit form

A registration and visit form where a QR code autofills the patient details. Staff scan instead of retyping, which cuts typing errors and speeds up each visit.

Offline stock dashboard

A private dashboard that works offline on Survey123 CSV exports. It reports by day and by location and calculates stock as opening plus restock minus use, with Excel and PDF export.

Campaign inventory tracker

For a three-month mobile health campaign that visits four locations in rotation, I built an Excel tracker. It records stock taken on each trip, stock used and remaining after it, and the running balance at the central store.

Screenshots are not published here because the data belongs to RHCI. I am happy to walk you through the work on a call.
