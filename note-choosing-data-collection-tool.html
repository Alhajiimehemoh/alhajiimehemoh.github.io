Choosing a data collection tool: Survey123, KoboToolbox, CommCare or ODK

A plain guide to picking a tool based on your team, your connectivity and what you need to do with the data.

21 October 2026 · Data collection · 1 min read

There is no single best tool. The right choice depends on your team, your budget and what happens to the data afterwards. These are the questions I ask first.

Who will build and maintain the forms?

If your team knows Excel, tools that use XLSForm let you design forms in a spreadsheet. Survey123, KoboToolbox and ODK all support XLSForm, so skills carry across.

Does the team work offline?

Most field work in Sierra Leone happens with weak or no signal. Check that the tool saves records on the phone and sends them later. Test this with real phones before you commit.

Where does the data need to go?

If your organisation already uses ArcGIS and maps, Survey123 fits naturally. If you need a free tool with simple exports to Excel or Power BI, KoboToolbox is a common choice. If you need follow-up of individual clients or community health workers over time, a case management tool such as CommCare may suit better.

What does it cost to run?

Look at licences, training, and the cost of mobile data and devices. A cheap tool that nobody can support is expensive.

Who owns the data?

Check where the data is stored and who can access it. Make sure you can export everything at any time.

Start small

Run a pilot with one form and a few users for a month. You will learn more from that than from any comparison table.
