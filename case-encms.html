The challenge

A growing college runs on many connected processes: admissions, fees, course registration, grading, results and student records. When these live in separate files and paper forms, students wait, staff repeat work and results are hard to protect.

Scope of the platform

AreaWhat it covers
AdmissionsOnline applications with an application fee
PaymentsBank payments with automatic confirmation, plus a slip or manual fallback
FeesInstalments, invoices, receipts and a minimum payment threshold before results are released
AcademicsSemester course registration with credits, GPA and CGPA, prerequisites, repeats and carry-overs, and attendance eligibility
ResultsAn approval chain with publication locking and controlled amendments
Wider campusStudent records, finance, payroll and HR, library, clinic, transport, ID and visitor management, notifications and reports

How results are approved

A phased rollout

  1. Start with current students and the processes that matter most.
  2. Prove it works and train staff.
  3. Add the wider campus modules step by step.
  4. Migrate old records once the new system is trusted.
Watch out

Rushing a system that holds grades and payments is risky. The plan is deliberately phased so each part is trusted before the next is added.

Built for the future

The platform is planned to be cloud-hosted, mobile-friendly with a mobile app, ready for more than one campus, with customisable roles and QR-verifiable documents, alongside a separate public website.
