CHW Card Studio

A system to issue, print and verify credential cards for Community Health Workers, so anyone can check a card is genuine.

Organisation
Rural Health Care Initiative (RHCI), Bo District
Role
Specification and design
Status
In design
Scale
More than 1,000 CHWs
Planned stack
Google Sheets, Google Apps Script, Google Drive
Audience
RHCI administrators, CHWs, facility staff and the public verifying a card

What it does

  • Gives each CHW a permanent ID
  • Produces standard CR80 cards with a QR code
  • Covers the card lifecycle: renewal, replacement, loss and revocation
  • Provides a public verification page that confirms a card is valid
  • Shows whether a CHW is Government-trained, MOMS-trained or dual-trained

Why Google tools

Google Sheets acts as the database, Apps Script as the backend, and Drive stores photos and QR images. RHCI staff already know these tools, and there are no servers to run or pay for.

Related work already built

I built a staff medical ID card generator as a single-file HTML app. It reads a staff and dependants workbook, prints front and back cards at 85.6 by 53.98 mm, and adds QR verification and security patterns. The CHW system applies the same ideas at larger scale.

Screenshots are not published here because the data belongs to RHCI. I am happy to walk you through the work on a call.
