Simple ways to protect your computer from viruses

Most computer problems can be avoided with a few safe habits and basic protection.

Cybersecurity · 1 min read

  • Install antivirus software
  • Avoid unknown flash drives
  • Do not open suspicious email attachments
  • Keep your system updated
  • Back up important files regularly

Cybersecurity does not have to be complicated. Awareness is the first line of defence.
