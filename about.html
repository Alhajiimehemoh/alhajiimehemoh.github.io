About me

I started in a computer training centre in Bo and now manage immunisation, home visit and malaria data for a health NGO.

I am Alhaji Ibrahim Mehemoh, based in Bo, Sierra Leone. I trained in computer software, hardware and networking, then spent years teaching Microsoft Office and fixing computers before moving into data and IT roles in government, politics and the NGO sector.

Since October 2024 I have worked at Rural Health Care Initiative (RHCI) as a Data Officer. I manage programme data for immunisation (EPI), home visits and malaria case management, build the Survey123 forms the field teams use, and supervise a contract Data Assistant. Working in health taught me that a good system is one that field staff actually use and managers actually trust.

I am studying Computer Science and I am open to consulting, contracts and full-time roles in M&E, health data and data systems. I work in English and Krio.
