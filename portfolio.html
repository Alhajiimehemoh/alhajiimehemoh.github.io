Earlier work

  • Health facility reporting tools, RHCI

    Problem: Manual reporting and inconsistent tracking reduced accuracy and slowed submissions.

    Solution: Excel tracking tools for MMS (multiple micronutrient supplements), CHW attendance, ultrasound reporting and inventory, with standard templates and a monthly reporting workflow.

    Outcome: More accurate data and smoother monthly submissions.

  • Office network and computer support

    Problem: Frequent downtime, connectivity issues and recurring device problems.

    Solution: Router configuration, LAN and Wi-Fi improvements, a device setup checklist and a regular support routine.

    Outcome: Better uptime, fewer repeat issues and smoother daily operations.

  • Vitamin Angels supply tracking

    Problem: Unclear stock visibility for MMS, Vitamin A and Albendazole distribution.

    Solution: Excel templates and printed logbooks for recording each distribution.

    Outcome: Clearer stock visibility and accountability.

How a project runs

The same steps on every project, so you always know what happens next.

  1. Understand the need

    Who reads the result, and what decision does it support?

  2. Design the data

    Forms, fields, tables and checks that match how the team really works.

  3. Build and test

    Validate against real exports before anyone relies on the result.

  4. Hand over and train

    Clear documentation, so your team can run it without me.
