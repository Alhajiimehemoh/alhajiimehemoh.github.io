How technology is transforming healthcare in Sierra Leone

Digital data collection, electronic reporting and better tracking are changing how facilities work.

Healthcare delivery in Sierra Leone is shifting from paper-based processes to digital systems. Facilities can now monitor performance with Excel tracking sheets, digital logbooks and centralised databases.

Programmes such as MMS distribution, CHW attendance and ultrasound reporting become much easier to manage once the data is organised.

What technology improves

  • Fewer data errors
  • Faster reporting
  • Better tracking of supplies
  • Stronger decisions at facility and district level

What still holds us back

Limited internet access, lack of equipment and too little IT training slow progress. With investment in skills and infrastructure, healthcare systems can become more efficient and responsive.

I will keep supporting digital transformation in healthcare through data systems, IT support and technical training.
