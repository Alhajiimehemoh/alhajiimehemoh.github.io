Common questions

How many people per session?

Small groups work best for hands-on practice. Tell me your numbers and I will suggest a plan.

Can training be in Krio?

Yes. I train in English and Krio.

How it works

  1. Check the starting point

    A short pre-test shows what people already know.

  2. Plan the session

    Objectives, a timed agenda and practice data with no real names.

  3. Teach by doing

    Short demonstrations, then hands-on practice with support.

  4. Measure and follow up

    A post-test, certificates where agreed, and a follow-up date.
