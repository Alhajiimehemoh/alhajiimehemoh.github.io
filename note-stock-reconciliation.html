A simple monthly stock reconciliation for clinics and programmes

Compare what the records say with what is on the shelf, then explain every difference.

21 October 2026 · Inventory · 1 min read

Stock records and shelves drift apart quickly. A monthly reconciliation keeps them honest.

What you need

  • Opening stock from last month's closing figure
  • Everything received, with dates and delivery notes
  • Everything issued, with who received it
  • A physical count done on the same day

The calculation

Opening stock plus received minus issued gives expected closing stock. Compare it with the physical count. Any difference must be explained.

Common causes of differences

  • Items issued but not recorded
  • Entry mistakes in quantities or units
  • Expired or damaged items not written off
  • Borrowing between locations without a record

Keep it practical

Do the count with two people. Record the difference, the reason and the action taken. Set a reorder level for each item so you order before you run out.

The stock tracker on this site calculates closing stock and flags items to reorder. It is a free Excel template you can adapt.
