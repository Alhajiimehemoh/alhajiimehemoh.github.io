Survey and form design

Most data quality problems start in the form. I build Survey123 and KoboToolbox forms that make the right entry the easy entry, and test them on real phones before your team goes to the field.

What you get

  • Choice lists and rules that stop bad entries

Good for: Field teams, campaigns and facility reporting

Common questions

Which tool should we use?

It depends on your team, your connectivity and where the data goes. I can help you choose between Survey123, KoboToolbox, ODK and CommCare.

Can you fix an existing form?

Yes. I can review your current XLSForm, fix errors and add validation without losing existing data structure.

How it works

  1. Map the questions

    Only the questions your reports will use, in a sensible order.

  2. Build the XLSForm

    Choice lists, skip logic, constraints and QR or lookup features.

  3. Test offline

    Real phones, no signal, then sync and check every record arrives.

  4. Train and launch

    A short session for collectors and a quick guide to keep.
