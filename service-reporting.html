M&E reporting

Good M&E reporting answers the same questions, the same way, every period. I help you define indicators clearly, track them against targets and turn them into reports people read.

Ask about thisWhatsApp me

Priced by scope after a short conversation. What affects the price

What you get

  • Indicator reference sheets everyone can follow
  • A tracker against targets
  • Clear monthly or quarterly reports
  • Slides for board and donor meetings

Good for: Programmes reporting to donors, boards or the DHMT

Common questions

Can you work with our existing logframe?

Yes. I can review it, tighten indicator definitions and build tracking around it.

Do you write donor reports?

I prepare the data, tables, charts and draft narrative for your team to review and submit.

How it works

  1. Define the indicators

    Numerator, denominator, source, frequency and owner for each one.

  2. Set up tracking

    A tracker with baselines, targets and progress by period.

  3. Write the report

    Highlights first, then indicators, changes, challenges and actions.

  4. Present it

    Slides for management, board or donor meetings.
