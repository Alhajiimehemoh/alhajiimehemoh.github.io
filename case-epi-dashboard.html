EPI reporting dashboard

A Power BI report that turns each monthly Survey123 immunisation export into the same trusted view, for people who need different levels of detail.

Organisation
Rural Health Care Initiative (RHCI), Bo District
Role
Data Officer: design, build and data quality
Status
In development
Data source
Survey123 EPI exports, supplied monthly
Tools
Power BI, Survey123, Excel
Audience
Nurses, health facilities, RHCI management, the DHMT, the RHCI Board and donors

The reporting question

EPI (immunisation) visit data is collected in Survey123 and exported as files. The challenge is to turn every new export into the same report without rebuilding it by hand, and to keep the numbers trustworthy as data volume grows.

What I built

A Power BI project built from the Survey123 EPI export, covering 1 January 2026 to date. It is designed to extend as each monthly export arrives, so the report grows instead of being rebuilt.

Different readers need different views. Nurses and facilities see their own work, RHCI management sees programme-wide results, and the DHMT, the Board and donors see summary reporting.

Data quality checks

Before numbers reach a dashboard, the records are checked. In March and April 2026 I ran a multi-stage analysis of the nurse datasets and produced a cleaned dataset. The checks cover:

  • GPS validation
  • Dose sequence violations
  • Duplicate detection
  • Village name inconsistencies
  • Compliance tracking per nurse

Each nurse received an Excel audit workbook, and management received consolidated Word reports and presentations.

How it is built

The work moves through staged checkpoints: foundation (data model and structure), build (measures and pages) and validation and handover (checking figures against the source export before release).

Screenshots are not published here because the data belongs to RHCI. I am happy to walk you through the work on a call.
